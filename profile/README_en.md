## About Us

The Yanshan University Student Network Information Association (YUNA) is a student organization dedicated to promoting computer science and technology at Yanshan University. We offer our members a broad platform for learning and hands-on practice, spanning web development, cybersecurity, server operations, high-performance computing, and more. Our goal is to cultivate technical talent and help students grow both technically and professionally.

## Departments

- **Development Department (YUNA devBranch)**: The Development Department focuses on web applications and software systems, while also taking charge of the association's internal network infrastructure and the exploration of emerging technologies. Guided by the principles of "project-driven learning" and "shared knowledge," we provide members with a complete growth path from first steps to real engineering practice. Whether you are new to programming or a developer eager to dig into low-level internals, you will find a direction that suits you here.

  - **Web Frontend Development**: Starting from web fundamentals and progressing to modern application development, members learn core technologies such as HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and master responsive layout and interaction design. They then move on to mainstream frameworks like Vue.js and React, grasp component-based development, and become able to build complex web applications independently.

  - **Web Backend & Full-Stack Development**: Members learn backend technologies such as Node.js, Python, and Java, covering server-side programming, database management, and API development. Through hands-on work with MySQL, Redis, and MongoDB, they come to understand data storage, business logic, and frontend–backend collaboration, gradually growing into full-stack developers capable of delivering complete projects.

  - **Engineering Practices & Software Collaboration**: The Development Department emphasizes training in real software engineering workflows. Members learn Git version control, collaborative development on GitHub/GitLab, code review, API specification design, and basic CI/CD pipelines — so that code not only "runs," but is maintainable, collaborative, and sustainable to iterate on.

  - **Project Practice & Technical Exploration**: We believe technical growth is driven by real projects. Members can contribute to the association's internal systems, campus applications, and computing competition entries, including the China Collegiate Computer Design Contest, "Internet+", and the Challenge Cup. We also encourage exploration of mobile development, automation scripting, open-source collaboration, and research into algorithms and systems — so that every idea has a chance to become a genuinely usable product.

- **Cybersecurity Department**: One of the association's earliest departments, formerly known as the CTF Department, the Cybersecurity Department is responsible for security education, CTF competition training, and security research. Our training centers on "security fundamentals" and "offensive and defensive practice": through theory, tooling, and hands-on challenges, we help members build a systematic body of security knowledge and explore the mechanisms that keep the networked world secure.

  - **Cybersecurity Fundamentals**: Beginning with computing fundamentals, members study Linux systems, security tooling, network protocols, and Python scripting. Mastering environments and common tools such as Kali Linux lays the groundwork for later vulnerability analysis and security research.

  - **CTF Competitions & Offensive/Defensive Practice**: The department has a long track record in security competitions and CTF events, covering web security, cryptography, reverse engineering, binary exploitation, and OSINT. Members solve security challenges in real competition settings, sharpening their ability to discover, analyze, and act on vulnerabilities.

  - **Vulnerability Research & Security Analysis**: Members explore web vulnerabilities, software security, and mobile security, studying how vulnerabilities arise, how they are exploited, and how to defend against them. Lab environments and practical projects deepen their understanding of real-world internet security problems.

  - **Competition Training & Technical Exchange**: The association has extensive competition experience, and our members have placed in security competitions. The department provides study materials, training guidance, and a forum for technical exchange, helping members at every skill level make steady progress.

  - **Exploring Security Technology**: Beyond competition training, the department encourages members to explore web crawling, security tool development, and security knowledge sharing — so that members can not only find problems, but also understand and help build a safer network environment.

- **Operations Department (YUNA Ops)**: The Operations Department keeps the association's technical services running reliably over the long term, serving as the essential link between what we build and how it is actually used. The department focuses on server administration, network infrastructure, automated operations, and platform maintenance, ensuring that every project is not merely built, but continues to serve its users reliably.

  - **Linux Services & System Administration**: Members learn core operations skills including Linux system administration, server deployment, permission management, log analysis, and troubleshooting. They take part in deploying and maintaining the association's website, application platforms, and internal services, gaining an end-to-end view of how a production system is run and kept healthy.

  - **Automation & Engineering Practice**: Members explore modern DevOps practices, learning Docker, CI/CD, Shell, and Python automation to make deployment and management more efficient. Automation removes repetitive toil and makes operational workflows faster and more reliable.

  - **Network & Infrastructure**: Members help build and manage domains, servers, network services, and various technical environments, providing a stable foundation for the association's projects. The department also supports other departments with deployment, environment configuration, and launch assistance, so that technical work reaches production smoothly.

  - **Platform Maintenance & Technical Support**: The department handles day-to-day operation of the association's existing services and campus platforms, including version updates, performance tuning, security maintenance, and incident response. Members gain experience in real service environments and build the ability to solve practical problems.

  - **Cloud Computing & Technical Exploration**: The department looks beyond maintaining existing systems and encourages members to explore cloud computing, containerization, networking, and DevOps engineering. From a single command on a server to a single response served to a user, the Operations Department works to keep technology genuinely reliable, so that what we create keeps delivering value.

- **Public Relations Department**: Combining the core responsibilities of the former Public Relations Department and the Secretariat, this department is the bridge between the association's members and its wider audience. It is responsible for the association's public image, cultural initiatives, event organization, and day-to-day administration, strengthening both our brand and our sense of team through content and coordination.

  - **Outreach & Brand Building**: The department runs the association's official platforms and plans their content, covering event promotion, visual design, announcements, and external communication. Members help shape the association's public image and learn how good content amplifies an organization's reach.

  - **Event Planning & Organization**: Members take part in planning, running, and reviewing association events, learning how to design event workflows, write proposals, and coordinate resources. Running real events builds organizational skill and project management experience.

  - **Internal Culture**: The department fosters the association's internal atmosphere and member relationships, encouraging cross-department exchange, team building, and a healthy organizational culture. Members strengthen their communication skills and their ability to work as a team.

  - **Well-Rounded Skill Development**: Joining the Public Relations Department means learning practical skills such as the Adobe design suite and copywriting, while also developing communication, coordination, and problem-solving abilities that carry over into future study, work, and teamwork.

  - **Connecting the Organization**: As a key coordinating department, Public Relations connects members across departments with external partners and resources, providing promotion, organization, and support for the association's events and technical projects, so that great work reaches a wider audience.

## Community & Career Development

We regularly host tech talks, hackathons, and social events to encourage exchange and collaboration. The association also offers career guidance, helping members find internships and job opportunities in the tech industry.
